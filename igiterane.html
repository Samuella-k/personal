Badge code key

Title band (top & bottom strip color)

Reverend Pasteur — senior pastors
Umukozi w'Imana — pastors & ministers
Umukristo — general attendee

Bottom strip pattern: dietary awareness

Triangles (matches top strip) = no restriction
Diamonds, gold = sugar-conscious (diabetes-aware catering)
Chevrons, slate = salt / oil-conscious (blood pressure-aware catering)
Alternating diamond & chevron = both

How to use

Glance at the bottom strip when serving food. Nothing on the badge names a condition — it just tells you which tray to steer toward.
